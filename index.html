Where they

Are

01 — Hvalfjörður, the west

Brekka

Three bedrooms — two doubles, two singles and a sofa bed — sleeping up to eight. Open-plan, underfloor heating, fibre internet, and a large terrace with a BBQ and a geothermal hot tub. Langholt stands 150 metres away.

45 min from Reykjavík · shop 3 km in summer, 25 km in winter

02 — Hvalfjörður, the west

Langholt

Built in 2013 and sleeping five — three bedrooms, two doubles and a single. The same fjord, the same underfloor heating and geothermal tub, on a hillside looking across at the Súlur range.

45 min from Reykjavík · 1.5 h from Keflavík airport

03 — The Westfjords

Tvera

Five bedrooms — three doubles, two singles — room for ten. Floor heating, a full kitchen, a terrace with a BBQ, and a geothermal pool in the rocks down at the shore. Dynjandi is thirty minutes north.

South coast of the Westfjords · Látrabjarg a day out

04 — Fnjóskadalur, the north

Hamragil

Six bedrooms — two doubles, two twins, two singles. Central heating, fibre internet, a large deck with a BBQ and a geothermal hot tub, in the birch valley behind Akureyri. Goðafoss is fifteen minutes on; Mývatn and Dettifoss a morning.

Near Akureyri · the north’s most dependable aurora skies

05 — The Golden Circle

Stekkar

Over 100 m² of living space plus a separate 30 m² guest house. Two bedrooms with double beds, a sofa bed if you need it, a long table under pendant lights and a wood stove in the lounge.

Geysir, Gullfoss and Þingvellir all within reach

What it costs

Brekka is €380 a night for six. Their booking page then adds €350.

That is the figure printed on the lodge’s own reservation panel today, before a single night is counted. Seven nights or more take 10% off the nightly rate; fourteen or more take 20%. Above six guests it is €33 per person per night.

Booked here, the fee is not there. You write to the people who own the houses, they answer, and the price you were quoted is the price you pay.
